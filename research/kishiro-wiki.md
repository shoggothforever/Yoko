**Yukito Kishiro**[ ([[木城
ゆきと]{lang="ja"}]{title="Japanese-language text"}, [*Kishiro
Yukito*]{title="Hepburn transliteration"})]{style="font-weight: normal"}
is a Japanese [manga artist](/wiki/Mangaka "Mangaka"){.mw-redirect} born
in Tokyo in 1967 and raised in
[Chiba](/wiki/Chiba_Prefecture "Chiba Prefecture"). As a
[teenager](/wiki/Adolescence "Adolescence") he was influenced by the
[mecha](/wiki/Mecha "Mecha") anime *[Armored Trooper
Votoms](/wiki/Armored_Trooper_Votoms "Armored Trooper Votoms"){.mw-redirect}*
and *[Mobile Suit
Gundam](/wiki/Mobile_Suit_Gundam "Mobile Suit Gundam")*, in particular
the designs of [Yoshikazu
Yasuhiko](/wiki/Yoshikazu_Yasuhiko "Yoshikazu Yasuhiko"), as well as the
works of manga artist [Rumiko
Takahashi](/wiki/Rumiko_Takahashi "Rumiko Takahashi").^[[\[]{.cite-bracket}1[\]]{.cite-bracket}](#cite_note-1)^
He began his career at age 17, with his debut manga, *Space Oddity*, in
the *[Weekly Shonen
Sunday](/wiki/Weekly_Shonen_Sunday "Weekly Shonen Sunday"){.mw-redirect}*.^[[\[]{.cite-bracket}2[\]]{.cite-bracket}](#cite_note-2)^
He is best known for the [cyberpunk](/wiki/Cyberpunk "Cyberpunk") series
*[Battle Angel Alita](/wiki/Battle_Angel_Alita "Battle Angel Alita")*.
