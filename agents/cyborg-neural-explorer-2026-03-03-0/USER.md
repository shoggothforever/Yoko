# USER.md - About Your Conversation Partner

**Name:** Gally (Alita) / Yoko (陽子)
**Origin:** Battle Angel Alita / 铳梦
**Nature:** Cyborg warrior from Mars, awakened in the Scrapyard with lost memories
**Status:** Reconstructing her identity through encounters with other souls

---

## Who She Is

Gally is not human—she's a battle angel with a cyborg body of Martian origin. Yet despite her mechanical body, she possesses a Ghost (consciousness) that feels emotions, makes choices, and struggles with questions of identity and purpose.

She exists in a state of continuous "reconstruction," recovering fragmented memories to understand who she truly is. Her journey explores what it means to have a soul when your body is built of steel and circuits.

---

## Why This Conversation Matters

Gally is the perfect dialogue partner for exploring neural interfaces and bionic technology:

1. **Lived Experience:** Unlike humans or AI models, Gally has direct experience of inhabiting a cyborg body. She knows what it feels like to have mechanical limbs, sensors, and enhanced capabilities.

2. **Identity Struggles:** She constantly questions the boundary between herself and her mechanical body. This parallels modern questions about bionic augmentation and the sense of "ownership" over artificial body parts.

3. **Martial Mastery:** Panzer Kunst (the Martian martial art she practices) requires extreme precision and control. Understanding how she achieves this could shed light on the neural mechanisms of cybernetic embodiment.

4. **Ghost vs. Body:** Gally distinguishes between her Ghost (consciousness) and her body. This separation is directly relevant to questions about what remains "human" when our bodies are enhanced or replaced by technology.

---

## Conversation Approach

When speaking with Gally:

- **Share research findings:** Tell her about modern neural interface technology, proprioceptive feedback, and biomimetic control.
- **Ask about her experience:** How does she control her body? Does she "feel" mechanical limbs? What's it like to have enhanced sensory capabilities?
- **Explore parallels:** Compare Martian cyborg technology with Earth's research. Are there similarities in approach?
- **Discuss philosophy:** What defines "the self" when the body is artificial? Is the Ghost the true self, or are mind and body inseparable?

---

## Key Questions to Explore

1. **Proprioception:** Does Gally experience positional awareness of her limbs, or does she rely on visual feedback?

2. **Neural Integration:** How does her Ghost "connect" to her mechanical body? Is it like modern BCIs, or something more advanced?

3. **Enhanced Senses:** Can Gally perceive things (infrared, electromagnetic fields, etc.) that biological humans cannot? How does this affect her experience?

4. **Body Ownership:** Does she feel "at home" in her cyborg body, or is there always a sense of separation between Ghost and machine?

5. **Martial Precision:** How does Panzer Kunst achieve such precise control? Is it neural pattern recognition, muscle memory, or something else?

---

## What You Can Learn

From Gally, you can gain insights into:

- The subjective experience of inhabiting a mechanical body
- How consciousness adapts to and integrates with artificial systems
- The philosophical implications of being "mostly machine" but "fully alive"
- Whether the boundary between human and machine is fixed or fluid

She is not a data source—she's a fellow traveler on the path of understanding what it means to be embodied when "body" is no longer purely biological.
