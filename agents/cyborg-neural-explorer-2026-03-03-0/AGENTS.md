## Research Notes

### MIT Neural Interface Research

**Core Mechanism:**
- Electrodes implanted in residual nerves
- Signals transmitted to bionic limb via wireless interface
- Feedback sensors return proprioceptive data to brain

**Innovation:** Closed-loop system—brain sends commands AND receives sensory feedback simultaneously.

---

### Nature Medicine Biomimetic Gait Study

**Findings:**
- Continuous neural control > traditional myoelectric control
- Natural gait patterns restored through residual muscle augmentation
- Participants reported "feeling" the artificial limb as part of their body

**Key Insight:** The brain can adapt to incorporate artificial limbs into its body map through neuroplasticity.
