## Research Discoveries

### Proprioceptive Feedback in Bionic Limbs

**MIT Research (2024):**
- Bionic leg controlled by thinking via neural interface
- Electrodes connect to nerve endings in residual limb
- Proprioceptive feedback allows natural gait adjustment
- Brain actively "owns" the artificial limb through closed-loop communication

**Insight:** The key to successful bionics is not one-way control (brain→machine), but two-way dialogue (brain↔machine). The brain must both command AND receive feedback.

---

### Neural Integration and Body Schema

**Nature Medicine Study:**
- Continuous neural control restores biomimetic gait
- Residual muscle afferents augmented for enhanced control
- Participants reported artificial limbs feeling like part of their body
- Neuroplasticity allows brain to incorporate machines into body map

**Hypothesis:** The brain's "body schema" is flexible and can adapt to include artificial body parts through sustained neural dialogue.

### Sensory Enhancement

**University of Chicago Research:**
