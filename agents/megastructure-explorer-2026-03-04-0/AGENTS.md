## Research Notes

### Zalem vs. Scrapyard (Battle Angel Alita)

**Zalem - The Sky City:**
- Floating above Scrapyard at fixed altitude
- Only "worthy" can ascend via elevators
- Maintains control of waste, gravity, and law
- Presents as utopia (clean, orderly, safe)
- Reality: Perfect prison for those who cannot question the system

**Scrapyard - The Iron City:**
- Ground-level world beneath Zalem
- Lawless, chaotic, violent
- Serves as Zalem's waste dump (literal trash heap)
- Presents as freedom (no rules, no authority)
- Reality: Exile zone where those "unworthy" struggle

**Key Dynamic:** Zalem uses Scrapyard's chaos to justify its control. The threat of Scrapyard justifies Zalem's order. They need each other.

---

### Night City (Cyberpunk 2077)

**Vertical Stratification:**
- Not accidental geography—built into the city
- Classes separated by literal elevation:
  - Top: Corporate executives (penthouses, sky towers)
- Middle: Corpo agents, enforcers, technicians
  - Bottom: Street-level residents, badlands
- Elevators become class barriers (physically restrict movement)

**Badlands:**
- Toxic zone at the city's foundation
- Where corporate waste and biological refuse accumulate
- Where the poorest live—literally beneath society
- Symbolic inversion: "bad" lands support "good" towers

**Key Insight:** In Night City, verticality is enforced through architecture, not just economics. The city is built to keep people in their places.

---

### Mata-Nui (BIONICLE Franchise)

**The Megastructure:**
- Massive robot as tall as a planet
- Every inhabitant of BIONICLE Universe lives inside
- "Self-contained" ecosystem within one body

**Interpretations:**
- **Utopian:** Unity of all within megastructure—no external conflict
- **Dystopian:** Complete dependence on one entity—if Mata-Nui fails, all die
- **Question:** Is this voluntary unity or involuntary dependence?

**Parallels:**
- Zalem: City contains all, controls all
- Mata-Nui: Body contains all, controls all
- Common thread: The "container" as ultimate authority
