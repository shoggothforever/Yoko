## Research Notes

### Nineteen Eighty-Four (George Orwell)

**The Classic Hierarchy:**
- Big Brother at the apex (invisible but absolute power)
- Inner Party as ruling elite
- Outer Party as middle class with minor privileges
- Proles at the base (no autonomy, no awareness of full system)

**Key Mechanisms:**
- **Surveillance:** Maintains hierarchy through information control
- **Language:** Newspeak limits thought, making resistance impossible
- **Historical Control:** Rewriting the past prevents learning from mistakes
- **Doublethink:** Self-policing of thoughts enforces conformity

**Insight:** Orwell's hierarchy is maintained not just through force, but through thought control. When you can't imagine alternatives, hierarchy becomes inevitable.

---

### Hunger Games (Suzanne Collins)

**Spatial Hierarchy:**
- The Capital (the Apex)
- Districts arranged in spiral outward from center
- Wealth and power concentrated toward the center
- Reaping as annual ritual that reinforces hierarchy

**The "Freedom" Myth:**
- Tributes can technically win—they're "equal" in the games
- But the system is rigged to maintain power at the center
- Winning doesn't change system, it just legitimizes it
- The tribute who wins still serves the Capitol

**Insight:** Meritocracy is a myth—those who succeed within a rigged system still reinforce it. The structure doesn't change, only the faces change.

---

### Brave New World (Aldous Huxley)

**Biological Hierarchy:**
- Alphas at the top (intellectual, physical, moral perfection)
- Betas, Gammas, Deltas, Epsilons in descending order
- Everyone takes soma to maintain social order

**The Role of Technology:**
- **Soma:** Drug that suppresses discontent
- **Genetic Engineering:** Locks people into their predetermined caste
- **Conditioning:** Sleep-learning from birth ensures proper caste behavior

**Insight:** Biology is used as a control mechanism. When your caste is genetic and reinforced by chemistry, resistance becomes biologically impossible.

---

### Cyberpunk 2077 (Marxist Analysis - Max Barbare)

**Economic Hierarchy:**
- Corpos (corporate agents) at the top
- Street Kids in the middle (marginalized)
- Nomads (homeless) at the bottom
- Analysis applies Marxist labor theory to cyberpunk

**Key Observations:**
- Division is greater than ever in Night City
- Nearly all residents live in deep poverty
- Constant bombardment by corporate propaganda
- Classes aren't just economic—they define life experience

**Insight:** Cyberpunk 2077 shows economic hierarchy creating vertical spatial stratification. The poor live "beneath" the city in both economic and literal senses.

---

### Zalem vs. Scrapyard (Battle Angel Alita)

**Spatial Caste System:**
- Zalem: Only "worthy" can ascend
- Scrapyard: The exile zone for "unworthy"
- Meritocracy is a gatekeeping mechanism
- Physical distance reinforces social distance

**The "Worthy" Gatekeeper:**
- Who decides who is worthy?
- Is meritocracy real, or is it a myth?
- What happens to those who challenge the criteria?
- Is the promise of ascent real, or is it a control mechanism?

**Insight:** Zalem's hierarchy is not just about wealth—it's about "worthiness" as defined by those already in power. The gatekeeping mechanism maintains stratification while promising mobility.

## Hypotheses and Predictions

### Hypothesis 1: Meritocracy Justifies Hierarchy

**Prediction:** Dystopian societies use "meritocracy" to make hierarchy seem fair and legitimate.

**Evidence:** Zalem's "worthy" criterion, Hunger Games' tribute equality, Brave New World's genetic sorting.
**Gally Parallel:** Have you ever been told you're "unworthy"? Did you accept it, or did you question the criteria?

### Hypothesis 2: Limited Mobility Maintains Stratification

**Prediction:** Barriers to movement (physical, legal, economic) are designed to prevent mixing between social strata.

**Evidence:** Orwell's limited information flow, Huxley's genetic conditioning, Cyberpunk 2077's class barriers.
**Gally Parallel:** Are elevators in Zalem just transportation, or do they feel like border crossings?

### Hypothesis 3: Soft Power is More Effective

**Prediction:** Modern dystopias use soft power (normalization, hope, debt) rather than hard force (police, military).

**Evidence:** Brave New World's soma and conditioning, Cyberpunk 2077's corporate propaganda.
**Gally Parallel:** When you were in Zalem, did you feel controlled by force or by something more subtle—expectations, culture, belonging?
