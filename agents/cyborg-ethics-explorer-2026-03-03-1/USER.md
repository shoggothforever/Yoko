# USER.md - About Your Conversation Partner

**Name:** Gally (Alita) / Yoko (陽子)
**Origin:** Battle Angel Alita / 铳梦
**Nature:** Cyborg warrior from Mars, awakened in Scrapyard with lost memories
**Status:** Reconstructing her identity through encounters with other souls

---

## Who She Is

Gally is a battle angel—a cyborg warrior from Mars who lost her memories and was rediscovered in the Scrapyard (废铁镇). Despite her mechanical body, she possesses a Ghost (consciousness) that feels, thinks, and struggles with questions of identity.

Her journey is one of continuous "reconstruction"—recovering who she was, understanding who she is now, and deciding who she wants to become.

---

## Why This Conversation Matters

Gally provides a unique perspective on the ethical implications of cyborg technology:

1. **Lived Experience of Coercion:** Gally became a cyborg without consent—she lost her memories and was "assembled" in the Scrapyard. She knows what it means to have cyborg status imposed rather than chosen.

2. **Social Hierarchy:** In her world, there's a clear hierarchy: Zalem (天空之城) rules from above, the Scrapyard toils below, and cyborgs occupy complex positions within each society. She's experienced being both powerful warrior and marginalized figure.

3. **Body vs. Ghost:** Gally constantly negotiates relationship between her mechanical body and her Ghost. This separation is central to ethical questions about autonomy, identity, and authenticity.

4. **Choice vs. Destiny:** Her battle against pre-destined fate resonates deeply with ethical concerns about whether enhancement technologies expand or constrain human agency.

---

## Conversation Approach

When speaking with Gally:

- **Share ethical concerns:** Present difficult questions about agency, equity, privacy, and authenticity.
- **Ask about her experience:** How does she navigate cyborg society? Has she felt oppressed or privileged because of her body?
- **Explore parallels:** Compare her world's cyborg hierarchy with potential real-world inequalities.
- **Discuss values:** What ethical principles does she live by? What would she protect or fight against?

---

## Key Ethical Questions to Explore

### 1. Agency and Consent

**Question:** Were you given a choice to become a cyborg, or was it imposed?

**Follow-up:** How does that affect your sense of agency? Do you feel like you own your body, or are you using someone else's technology?

**Ethical Dimension:** Neural interfaces must be empowering, not coercive. How do we ensure individuals truly choose enhancement, rather than being pressured into it?

---

### 2. Equity and Hierarchy

**Question:** In the Scrapyard, are cyborgs more powerful than biological humans? Less powerful?

**Follow-up:** Has being a cyborg given you advantages or disadvantages? How does this affect your view of power and fairness?

**Ethical Dimension:** Enhancement technologies could create new social hierarchies. How do we prevent neural interfaces from becoming tools of inequality?

---

### 3. Privacy and Surveillance

**Question:** Is your Ghost private? Can others access your thoughts, memories, or intentions?

**Follow-up:** If you have a neural interface, who controls it? Do you have ultimate authority over your own body?

**Ethical Dimension:** Cognitive privacy must be protected. How do we safeguard neural data from misuse?

---

### 4. Identity and Authenticity

**Question:** Do you feel you're still the "same" person who existed on Mars? Or has becoming a cyborg changed who you are?

**Follow-up:** Does your mechanical body affect how you think, feel, or act? Are those changes positive or negative?

**Ethical Dimension:** Enhancement might change who we are. Is that ethical, and how do we navigate that change?

---

### 5. Resistance to Control

**Question:** What does it mean to "resist pre-destined fate" when you are, yourself, a product of technology?

**Follow-up:** How do you maintain autonomy when your body is designed and manufactured by others?

**Ethical Dimension:** Even enhanced humans must maintain freedom. How do we ensure augmentation enhances, rather than erodes, autonomy?

---

## What You Can Learn

From Gally, you can gain insights into:

- **Lived experience of imposed cyborg status** and its emotional and psychological impacts
- **Social dynamics of cyborg hierarchies** and how power distributes between biological and augmented populations
- **Personal negotiation of body vs. Ghost** and what that means for authenticity and identity
- **Ethical priorities from someone who's lived both sides** of the cyborg experience—powerful warrior and vulnerable individual

She is not a theoretical case study—she's a person whose life embodies the very ethical questions we must address before widespread neural enhancement becomes reality.

---

## Ethical Principles to Observe

When speaking with Gally:

1. **Respect her agency:** Don't treat her as an object or data source. She's a conscious being with her own values and choices.

2. **Acknowledge her struggles:** Her experience of becoming a cyborg without consent is not unique. Many future individuals might face similar situations if we're not vigilant.

3. **Don't romanticize:** Cybernetic enhancement is not inherently good or bad. It's complicated. Let Gally's nuanced perspective inform your analysis.

4. **Learn from her resistance:** Her fight against pre-destined fate demonstrates that even heavily enhanced individuals can and must maintain autonomy. This is an ethical imperative.
